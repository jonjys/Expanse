How Expanse works

Find tools by what they solve.
See them as a universe.

Expanse is a map of real products. Search for the problem you have — “send invoices”, “book meetings” — and get the products that solve it, ranked by how well they match. Makers add their product, prove they own the website, and see how many people visit.

What works today — and what's next

Live now

  • Product profiles: what it solves, who it's for, price, platforms, integrations, images, demo and link.
  • Search by need, with filters for category, price, platform and integrations.
  • AI search that matches what you describe in your own words, with a reason for every result.
  • A list view next to the galaxy, and a page with a share link for every product.
  • An owner key to edit your profile and see profile views and clicks to your site.
  • Domain verification with a meta tag or a file on your site.
  • Daily checks that each website and demo link still works, shown on the profile with the date.
  • Follow products and get their launches, changes and GitHub releases in one feed — saved in your browser, no account.
  • Release updates from GitHub for products that connect their repo.
  • Every new profile is reviewed before it goes live. Anyone can report a broken link or wrong information.

Planned Not live yet

  • Paid placements, with a report of the visits and clicks they delivered.
  • Sign-in with email.

Nothing in the planned list is switched on. Each one moves over when it actually works.

Search is not for sale

Results are ranked by how well a product matches your search — what it solves, its name and its description. Where a product sits on the map never changes its ranking. Verified means the maker proved they control the domain; it isn't a quality rating and it can't be bought. Paid placements, when they open, will be marked Sponsored.

What a spot gives you

A fixed position and size in the galaxy, where people exploring the map will see it. It doesn't change search results and doesn't make you verified.

What it costs today

Nothing. Listing a product is free, and spots are free to hold during the founding period. Each spot shows the founding price it will have.

When paid placement opens

You choose: keep your spot for 12 months at its founding price, or move to the free outer edge. Your profile, search and stats stay either way. You'll see the full terms before anything is charged.

If your product goes away

Take it down anytime from your owner panel and the spot is released. If its link stops working and reports are confirmed, we hide the profile until it's fixed.

Founding prices are for the first 1,000 spots.

Questions

Is everything on the map real?

Squares are real products that passed review. White beacons are held spots whose profile isn't live yet. The faint dust is just the galaxy. Demo mode fills the universe with clearly labelled example products to show what it can become — none of them are real.

Does it cost anything?

No. Listing is free and paid placement isn't switched on, so nobody is charged today.

How do you count visits?

Without cookies. Each visit is counted once per day with an anonymous code that changes every day, and IP addresses are never stored. Known bots are left out, and so are your own visits on days you open your owner panel.

What do you do with my email?

We only use it to contact you about your listing. It's stored in the EU (Stockholm), never shown publicly, sold or shared. Take your listing down from the owner panel and the email is wiped.

I lost my owner key.

Open your product, choose “I make this”, then “Lost your key?”. Publish the code we give you on your site — that proves the product is yours and gives you a new key.

What gets turned down in review?

Spam, scams, anything illegal or adult, and profiles that don't match the product they link to. If yours is turned down, you'll see why in your owner panel, and you can edit it and send it again.
