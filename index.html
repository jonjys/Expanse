FAQ

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Claiming a square is free while Expanse is in preview. No card, no signup to look around.

Is this a social network?

No. You never post on Expanse. Your square mirrors what you already do elsewhere, and every click goes out to your own shop, channel, repo or calendar.

What do you get access to?

Read-only access to the accounts you choose to connect, so your square can show public numbers like commits, posts or sales. We never post for you, and you can disconnect anytime.

What do you do with my email?

Leaving your email is optional. If you do, we use it for one thing: telling you when founding claims open. It's stored in the EU (Stockholm), never sold or shared, and you can have it deleted anytime by replying to any email from us.

Do I own my square?

Your square is your spot on the map for as long as you use it. It's a place to be seen — not property, not an investment, and not something to trade. You can move, rename or remove it whenever you like.

What if I go quiet?

Your square fades. If it stays quiet for months, the spot can be freed for someone new — we email you before that happens.

Is everything on the map real?

Not yet. The map you see is a preview filled with example squares, so you can feel how it works. Things will change as we build it — founding squares get to shape it with us.
