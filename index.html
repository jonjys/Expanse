How Expanse works

A universe of people.
One square each.

Every glowing square is someone — a shop, a band, a startup, a freelancer. You claim a spot, plug in what you already use, and your square lights up every time you ship. No feed. No posting. No followers needed.

SellA price tag on the map. Leads with Buy, straight to your own checkout.
CreatePulses every time you post. Leads with your latest piece.
BuildA neon edge that flares on fresh commits. Leads with what just shipped.
Hire meGreen when you're free, red when you're booked. Leads with Book.

Location is everything

The universe grows from the core out. The closer you are, the more people fly past you — and the fewer spots are left. Prices are per cell, so a 2×2 is four cells and an 8×8 landmark is sixty-four.

Questions

Do I pay now?

No. Reserving a spot costs nothing today. When founding claims open, you get first access at the founding price you reserved — or you simply let it go.

What does “founding” mean?

The first 1,000 reserved spots. They get founding prices and are marked as founding spots on the map.

Is this a social network?

No. You never post on Expanse. Your square mirrors what you already do elsewhere, and every click goes out to your own shop, channel, repo or calendar. We only ever get read-only access to what you choose to connect.

Do I own my square?

Your square is your spot on the map for as long as you use it. It's a place to be seen — not property, not an investment, and not something to trade.

What if I go quiet?

Your square fades. If it stays quiet for months, the spot can be freed for someone new — we email you before that happens.

What do you do with my email?

One thing: tell you when founding claims open. It's stored in the EU (Stockholm), never sold or shared, and you can have it deleted anytime by replying to any email from us.

Is everything on the map real?

The universe you see is a preview of what Expanse will look like. White beacons are real founding reservations, and everything marked LIVE in the feed is real.
