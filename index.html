The Spotlight: the middle is earned

The 8 places in the core of the galaxy go to the products people like most — measured as the share of visitors who click through to the product's site or upvote it over the last 30 days, not as how many visitors it has. A big audience or a famous name doesn't buy the middle, and a new product starts at the average instead of at zero. 2 of the 8 places always go to the best products that went live in the last 30 days, and ties rotate every day instead of going to whoever came first. To qualify, a product needs a verified domain and a website that answers. Nobody is invited and no place is for sale: the maker's own visits and votes don't count. It updates every hour, and while fewer than 8 products qualify, every one that does gets a place.

Search is not for sale

Results are ranked by how well a product matches your search — what it solves, its name and its description — and then by how well liked it is: the share of visitors who click through or upvote over the last 30 days, not how many visitors it has. A well-liked product can pass a slightly closer match, never a much closer one, and ties rotate every day. Where a product sits on the map never changes its ranking. Verified means the maker proved they control the domain; it isn't a quality rating and it can't be bought. Sponsored products are marked Sponsored, show in at most two cards above the results only when they fit what you searched for, and never change the ranking below them.

Your spot on the map

Each product has one connected square: 1×1, 2×2, 4×4 or 8×8. One company may list several genuinely different products. Duplicate profiles for the same product are not allowed; choose a larger square rather than several copies of one listing.

Every product lives somewhere in the galaxy. Your position changes where people encounter your product while exploring; it never changes search ranking. Prices are per cell, so a 2×2 is four cells and an 8×8 landmark is sixty-four. Free profiles use a 1×1 Edge spot. You can choose another zone and size and pay after approval for 12 months of placement. Existing founding holds are preserved.

How payment works

After your product is approved, open My product (“Mine” on phones) → Map spot. Check the zone, size, full price and 12-month term, then pay securely with Stripe. Chosen spots are held during review and for 7 days after approval. Without payment they return to a free 1×1 Edge spot. Your 12-month placement starts when payment is confirmed. No automatic renewal. When the term ends, your product continues on a free 1×1 Edge spot. No visits, search positions or sales are guaranteed.

If your product goes away

Take it down anytime from your owner panel and the spot is released. If its link stops working and reports are confirmed, we hide the profile until it's fixed.

Questions

Is everything on the map real?

Squares are real products that passed review. White beacons are held spots whose profile isn't live yet. The faint dust is just the galaxy. Demo mode fills the universe with clearly labelled example products to show what it can become — none of them are real.

Who gets the middle of the map?

The products people use most. The 8 places in the core — the Spotlight — go to live products with a verified domain and a working website, ranked by the share of visitors who click through or upvote over the last 30 days. 2 places are kept for products that went live in the last 30 days. Nobody is invited and you can't buy a place.

Can big names or money buy their way up?

No. Rankings use the share of visitors who like a product, not the number of visitors, so a big audience doesn't win by size. New products start at the average, not at zero, newcomers always get 2 places in the Spotlight, and ties rotate every day instead of going to whoever came first. Payment can buy a chosen map placement or a card marked Sponsored under the same published terms for everyone. It never buys search ranking or Spotlight. Available map positions are first come, first served; payment never changes search relevance. Our own products follow exactly the same rules.

Does it cost anything?

Listing is free, including a 1×1 Edge spot. You can optionally pay once for 12 months of chosen map placement, from €4, after your product is approved. Sponsored cards are a separate paid option. Both follow the same terms for everyone.

How do you count visits?

Without cookies. Each visit is counted once per day with an anonymous code that changes every day, and our event tables do not store raw IP addresses. Hosting providers may process network addresses in security logs. Known bots are left out, and so are your own visits on days you open your owner panel. Upvotes work without an account too: your browser keeps a random ID and we store only a scrambled version of it, so each browser has one vote per product.

What do you do with my email?

We use it for your listing, sign-in codes and support. Listing data is stored in the EU (Stockholm). Your email is never shown publicly or sold; our service providers are described below. Take your listing down from the owner panel and its contact email is wiped.

I lost my owner key.

Open your product, choose “I make this”, then “Sign in with email” — we send a code to the address you added the product with. No access to that inbox? Choose “Lost your key?” and publish the code we give you on your site: that proves the product is yours and gives you a new key.

What gets turned down in review?

Spam, scams, anything illegal or adult, and profiles that don't match the product they link to. If yours is turned down, you'll see why in your owner panel, and you can edit it and send it again.

Privacy and rules

Who runs Expanse

Expanse is made by NyttoLabs in Sweden. Questions, a data request or anything else: use the contact form below and we answer by email.

What we store

Product profiles (public), the maker's email (private, only for messages about the listing and sign-in codes), daily visit counts using salted hashes rather than storing raw IP addresses in event tables, upvotes (linked only to a scrambled random ID from your browser), and reports. Saved products, followed products, your vote list, your owner key and your personal activity history stay in your own browser. My space does not create a public profile or sync across devices. You can clear local activity from its History tab; clearing browser storage also removes local collections.

Who helps us run it

Supabase stores listing data in Stockholm, Vercel serves the site, Anthropic generates optional profile drafts and stack suggestions, Resend sends listing emails and Stripe processes optional payments. Their logs and subprocessors may involve international processing; see the privacy notice. Card details stay with Stripe and are never stored by Expanse. Nothing is sold, and there are no ad trackers.

The rules

Listings are for real software and tools, added by the people who make or represent them. Spam, scams, illegal or adult content and profiles that don't match their product are turned down or removed. You can edit or take down your listing anytime, and taking it down wipes your email.
