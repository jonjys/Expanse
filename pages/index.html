Questions

Is Expanse a social network?

No. You never post here. You connect Gumroad, GitHub, YouTube, your shop or your calendar, pick a mode, and the square mirrors what you already do. Everything points outward to your own platforms.

What's a mode?

Sell, Create, Build or Hire me. It sets how your square renders on the map (price tag, pulse, neon edge, status dot) and what the card leads with (Buy, Latest, Ask, Book). Set automatically from what you connect, switch any time.

Can I grow without paying?

Yes. Likes (+10), direct visits (+25) and updates (+50) fill the bar. Every full bar is one free cell you place yourself. A busy free square can outgrow a quiet €64 one.

What if I stop using it?

After 30 days of no activity the glow fades, after 60 the XP drains, after 90 days without answering reminders the square goes to public auction. Connect one account and keep doing what you do; that's enough.

Do I own it?

Yes, as long as it's alive. Resell to anyone at any price; Expanse takes 5–10% of the trade. Landmarks can also be leased out for events.
